# KUPURI CREATIVE STUDIO GAUNTLET

Every public release must pass all gates.

## Benchmark bar
- Pentagram — work leads; authorship and craft are visible.
- Wolff Olins — the strategic idea is legible behind the identity.
- Interbrand — creativity connects to business meaning without fake metrics.
- Landor — strategy becomes a coherent brand experience.
- COLLINS — unmistakable point of view; no default agency template language.
- WPP Creative — connected capabilities without confusing the buyer.
- Superside — production scale does not lower the creative bar.
- Clay — brand, digital product and website feel like one system.

## Hard failures
- Poppins/Inter/Geist used as an unexamined default.
- Cyan/blue SaaS wash, glassmorphism, gradient headline, pill soup, card-grid service page.
- Empty portfolio claims.
- Fake or unsupported metrics.
- Calling prototypes “launched” or forms “live” without verification.
- Generic copy: innovative, elevated, transformative, cutting-edge, unlock, seamless, tailor-made, next-level.
- More than one primary CTA per decision point.
- Motion without narrative or usability purpose.
- Desktop-only navigation.
- Public AI-agent positioning replacing the studio proposition.

## Required checks
1. Desktop screenshot at 1440px.
2. Mobile screenshot at 390px.
3. Keyboard navigation and visible focus.
4. Reduced-motion behavior.
5. The Extractor audit on public copy/source.
6. Broken-link check.
7. Performance pass; hero media must not block first meaningful content.
8. Case-study evidence labels: SHIPPED / PREVIEW / IN DEVELOPMENT / CONCEPT.
9. Contact flow tested end-to-end before launch.
10. Human review before production/domain changes.

## Release threshold
Overall >= 8.5/10.
No category under 7.5.
Any hard failure blocks release.
